# CampusGigs - pitch script (fictional example)

**Problem.** Students who need on-campus jobs waste hours chasing listings that are already filled. Last semester one student we interviewed spent 6 hours a week for a month checking boards and sent 14 cold emails to get two replies.

**Customer evidence.** We interviewed 23 students and 6 hiring staff. 15 of 23 students said they had applied to at least one listing that was already filled; 4 of 6 staff told us they forget to take posts down. One lab manager said: "Last month I got 40 emails for a job I filled in week one."

**Solution & MVP.** Our MVP is a shared spreadsheet plus a form: staff mark a job as filled in one click. We ran a 3-week pilot with 2 departments and ran usability tests with 8 students.

**Traction.** 140 sign-ups on our waitlist, 2 departments in the pilot, and one letter of intent from Career Services.

**Market.** There are about 4,000 colleges in the US. Bottom-up: 1,200 mid-size universities x $3,000 per year = a $3.6M first market.

**Business model.** Universities pay a subscription of $250/month per campus; students use it free.

**Competition.** Today students use the official job board, Handshake, and word of mouth. Unlike Handshake, we only show jobs that are still open, because staff close them in one click.

**Team & ask.** Our team is three CS students who all work campus jobs. We are raising $50k for a 6-month runway. Milestone: 10 paying campuses.
